How it works

From “what’s for dinner” to one list

Stovely works with your recipes, not a built-in library. It feels like a tidy notepad on the fridge, not a big meal-kit service.

  1. Add your recipes

    Type the ingredients, paste them in, or import a recipe by pasting a link from a recipe website.

  2. Pick this week’s meals

    Tap the circle next to the recipes you’re cooking. Cooking for more or fewer? Change the servings.

  3. Shop one list

    Matching ingredients are combined and sorted by aisle. Tick items off as you shop, or share the list with someone.

A look inside

Simple on purpose

Three screens do the job: your recipes, the weekly list, and the recipe you’re cooking.

What you get

Small things that save trips

Everything is built around one job: turning the meals you plan into a list you can actually shop from.

Adds it all up

½ cup of milk in one recipe and 1½ cups in another become 2 cups on your list. Change a recipe’s servings and the amounts scale to match.

Sorted by aisle

Produce, dairy, meat, pantry, and more, so you’re not zigzagging through the store.

Import from a website

Paste a recipe link and Stovely fills in the ingredients and steps. If a site blocks it, paste the recipe text instead.

Your recipes, your way

Keep the steps with each recipe, add a photo or pick an illustration, and open it while you cook.

Share the list

Send the list to a partner or roommate with Android’s share sheet.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your recipes live on your phone.

Questions

Good to know

Is Stovely available yet?

Not yet. It’s coming soon to Google Play for Android phones. There is no iPhone version at the moment.

Does it come with recipes?

No. Stovely works with the recipes you add yourself, so your list always matches what you actually cook.

Where are my recipes stored?

On your phone. Stovely has no accounts and no servers for your recipes, so we never see them. You can save a backup from the More screen.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your recipes and lists work without a connection. You only need to be online to import a recipe from a website or to buy Plus.

Which recipe websites can it import from?

Most sites that publish standard recipe data. Some sites block apps, and Stovely will tell you when that happens so you can paste the recipe text instead.

Email hello@stovely.app.
